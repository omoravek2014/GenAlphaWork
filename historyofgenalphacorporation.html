Our Story

GenAlpha Workbooks was created to encourage children to learn more and focus on their studies. The following timeline describes the history of our corporation.

2016 – The Beginning

Our business was founded in 2016 in Úholičky by a 12-year-old founder known as Ing. Tung Tung Sahurr. The original story also mentions efforts to support families affected by a condition called Tungsahur disease, including a proposed contribution of $2,000 toward treatment.

2017 – New Achievements

In 2017, the website claimed an award as the most kind-hearted website on Earth. It also reported receiving $340,000 in advance. During the same year, the website received many messages from visitors. This encouraged the team to update older features and renew parts of the website.

2020 – A Difficult Period

In 2020, the COVID-19 pandemic began affecting everyday life around the world. Online classes became increasingly important, and parents often had to help their children with schoolwork at home. This created new challenges for the website. Our original story describes this period as a difficult time for the corporation.

2022 – The Comeback

In 2022, GenAlpha Workbooks made what the original story describes as its biggest comeback in the history of the website. The project continued developing and introducing improvements.

And the Story Continues!

GenAlpha Workbooks continues to focus on making learning more interesting, accessible and enjoyable. We believe that learning should be an adventure filled with curiosity and creativity!
