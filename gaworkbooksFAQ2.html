Answer to "How can i sign up my child?"

It's very simple! First, under the "About our business" button, you'll see a "Sign In" button. When you click on it, two buttons will show. The first one is "Sign in as Parent" and the second one is "Sign in as child". Click "Sign in your child", and fill in the details about your son/daughter. Once it's all finished, we will send you a message for signing in + some instructions for the process.
