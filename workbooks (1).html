About Our Interactive Workbooks

GenAlpha Workbooks provides interactive school practice for students in Grades 1–9. The workbooks are designed to support different learning needs and offer a variety of ways to practice school subjects.

Each grade workbook is planned to include:

  • 400 exercises in total.
  • 40 exercises in each of 10 subjects.
  • Multiple-choice questions.
  • True-or-false exercises.
  • Fill-in-the-blank questions.
  • Automatic answer checking.
  • Explanations and progress tracking.

Students can work through the exercises at their own pace and return to their learning whenever they are ready.
