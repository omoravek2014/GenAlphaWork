Sign Up As Child - Explanation

In case you don't understand, this button has a different function. You have to sign in, and then the parent's bluetooth connection will pop up at the top of your screen. Click accept and the parent has saved you as their child. The parent will add you to the "Children's Folder" shortly after.

Example:

The Parent will be logged in the website. The child will have the same instructions as the parent. Once finished, a connection will pop up and the child HAS to click accept. This is because that the parent has made a bluetooth connection through the device, and the child's device will recieve a connection from the parent.
