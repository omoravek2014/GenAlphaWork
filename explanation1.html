Sign Up As Parent - Explanation

In case you don't understand, this button is intended to add your child to your "Children's Folder". Once you're done signing in, you will recieve a setting that will allow you to add your child to your Child Requests.

Example:

The Parent sets a timer to 1 hour for GenAlpha learning. Once the timer sets, the child can go back to doing their thing.
